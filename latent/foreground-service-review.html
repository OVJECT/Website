LATENT · com.ovject.latent · version 1.0.0 (9)

Nearby Bluetooth connection: foreground service demonstration

LATENT is a nearby messenger. Its connectedDevice foreground service keeps the Bluetooth peer link available for incoming messages and relay traffic while the app is off screen. The user can turn background operation off in Me → Keep nearby link on.

Direct MP4 video · Jump to the settings demonstration (2:58)

  1. First use: allow nearby-device access after the app's age and usage-rule checks. The app creates a local identity; no login credentials are required.
  2. Allow notifications. After returning to Android's home screen, “Nearby link is on” remains visible as an ongoing notification.
  3. Open Me and turn Keep nearby link on off. Leave the app; the foreground service stops and its notification disappears.
  4. Return to the app and turn the setting on. Leave again; the connected-device service and ongoing notification remain active.

Recorded on a disposable Android emulator using an APK generated from the submitted build 9 App Bundle. The two recordings are joined in their original order and timing. This demonstrates actual permission, notification and foreground-service behavior; it is not a physical two-phone radio-range or message-delivery test. Internet relay use was disabled before first registration, and no real user's account or messages appear.

The same age, consent and permission checks apply to all users. There is no reviewer-only behavior. The service was separately verified as foreground type connectedDevice with notification 4101 when enabled, and absent after background operation was disabled and the app left the screen.
