Privacy policy

Identity and friends

The app creates a cryptographic identity on your device and stores your display name, avatar and friends’ public contacts. It does not read your address book or require a phone number. Your recovery key uses secure device storage when available, with a fallback file in app storage when necessary. It is not part of a friend invitation QR. On Android, the identity is also stored in Google Block Store for reinstall recovery with cloud backup disabled. A user-initiated operating-system device-to-device restore may transfer that identity to another device.

Messages and public spaces

Direct and group messages are encrypted in transit. Messages and friend information are stored in an encrypted device database. Game records, avatars and some settings use separate local storage. Echo posts are public: nearby people can read and relay them. Echo masks change daily; blocking a mask blocks future reception from that mask. Arena room announcements include a daily identifier, friend-recognition tag, game type and timestamp and are sent over a public internet channel. Your profile display name is not sent in room announcements. Deleting your copy cannot recall content another person has already saved or copied.

Purposes, data items and legal bases

Service delivery: Device-generated identity, display name, avatar, friend contacts, conversations and game records are processed as needed for the chat, play and restoration features you request. Device-only data is distinguished from operator-server processing. Necessary service processing relies on Article 15(1)(4) of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act. Internet connections: When you choose internet relay use, account identifiers, public signature-verification keys, authentication information, inbox addresses, connection IPs and encrypted delivery data support authentication, delivery and troubleshooting. Recovery keys and private/group conversation decryption keys are not supplied to the relay. Support and rights requests: Reply addresses, request scope and minimum necessary evidence support assistance, ownership verification, access, correction and deletion requests. Reports are reviewed only as needed for safety and harm prevention. Processing information about other people requires assessment of Article 15(1)(6), possible impact on their rights and removal of unnecessary material. Optional features: Separate QR SDK diagnostics are described below.

Retention and disposal

Device data: Conversations, friends and identity remain until the relevant deletion/reset. Reset and account deletion also clear related avatars and game records. Non-identity choices such as language, accepted rules and the passed age threshold may remain. Incomplete received envelopes older than seven days are cleaned during startup/reception. Relay account: Authentication/connection data remains until deletion is processed. Verified owner deletion removes the account, role, registration state and queued mailbox. A minimal replay-prevention marker lasts 20 minutes and is then cleaned on a 60-second maintenance cycle. Offline mailbox: A seven-day expiry applies to disconnected persistent sessions. It is not a seven-day deadline measured from creation of every individual message. New service logs: Seven-day retention with hourly rotation/expiry, normally cleared within eight days. Rate-limit IPs are used only in a 60-second in-memory request window and cleaned every 60 seconds. Legacy test logs: The retention transition was applied on September 15, 2026; default system journals use a 30-day policy and inactive Mosquitto files expire 30 days after last modification. Including existing syslog rotations, pre-transition records are normally cleared within 40 days of transition (by October 25, 2026). Missed cleanup runs resume when the server restarts. Support/reports: Keep only as needed to handle the case, then delete within 30 days of completion. Unnecessary material is removed sooner. Operator-managed mail, attachments, trash and downloaded copies are included. Legally required exceptions identify their basis, scope and end date. Erasure: Electronic records, files, accounts and sessions are deleted or expired. Other users’ devices, exports, operating-system device transfers and providers’ independent records are separate. Uninstalling or deleting an account does not instantly physically erase all external copies. Rights/deletion requests for those records can be directed to the operator or the relevant provider.

The app uses encryption for direct/group message transport and the conversation database, along with secure device storage and app storage. Some information, including avatars, game records and settings, is held in separate local storage. This does not guarantee separate encryption of every file or prevent access by someone who controls your device. Keep device locking and operating-system updates enabled. Default relay connections use TLS and per-account inbox access control. Refer to the operator disclosures for the actual retention and disposal of connection and other operational logs.

Children’s personal information

LATENT is for people aged 14 and over and does not offer registration to users under 14. A birth date entered at first launch is used on the device to check eligibility, then cleared from the input fields. It is not saved in app storage or sent to a server. Only the passed age threshold stays on the device and survives an identity reset. If the check fails, identity creation, restoration and chat connections do not start. Only a retry time is retained to limit repeated attempts; it is removed when the app is reopened after 24 hours. Clearing app data also removes the age receipt. This is a self-declared age check, not verification of identity documents. Unnecessary information about a child under 14 received through support or reports is deleted. Any further necessary processing follows applicable legal requirements.

Terms of use

Service age

You must be at least 14 to use LATENT. Enter your birth date accurately. Identity creation and chat connections begin after the age check and acceptance of the usage rules. Registration with parental consent for users under 14 is not offered.

Prohibited conduct

Harassment, hate or discrimination, physical threats, scams, impersonation, exposing private information, sexual exploitation, illegal content and unsolicited advertising are prohibited. Respect other people’s rights and privacy, and their choice to end a conversation or game.

Using Echo

Echo is public to nearby users. Do not post contacts, home addresses, recovery keys or sensitive information. Bypassing public safety filters or changing identities to evade another person’s block is prohibited.

Protect your identity

Anyone with your recovery key can use the same identity. Never share recovery keys or transfer QR codes with others. A friend QR contains a public contact, not your recovery key. Verify friends using a QR code or identity fingerprint.

Connection and storage limits

Delivery depends on distance, Bluetooth availability, operating-system background restrictions and internet conditions. Delivery times and continuous connections are not guaranteed. LATENT does not replace emergency communication services. Keep your own copies of important information.

Reporting and action

Hold a message to report or block its sender. Blocking applies to future reception on this device. Send report details to the support contact to request review or appeal. The operator cannot recall all distributed offline copies. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in reports.

Child safety standards

No child sexual abuse or exploitation

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), text promoting exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation and exploitation transactions are strictly prohibited. Requesting, promoting, sharing or linking to this content is also prohibited.

How to report

Hold the message to report or block, and contact support. Do not download or forward illegal images or videos. You can report using a description and the subject’s identifier without attaching content. Contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.

Operator response

The operator reviews received reports and takes action required by applicable law within its control. Confirmed child sexual abuse material is reported to the appropriate authorities where legally required, with cooperation on valid requests. The operator cannot recall all distributed copies or completely prevent someone from returning under a new identity. Contact support for a response or appeal.
