Privacy policy

Identity and friends

The app creates a cryptographic identity on your device and stores your display name, avatar and friends’ public contacts. It does not read your address book or require a phone number. Your recovery key uses secure device storage when available, with a fallback file in app storage when necessary. It is not part of a friend invitation QR. On Android, the identity is also stored in Google Block Store for reinstall recovery with cloud backup disabled. A user-initiated operating-system device-to-device restore may transfer that identity to another device.

Messages and public spaces

Direct and group messages are encrypted in transit. Messages and friend information are stored in an encrypted device database. Game records, avatars and some settings use separate local storage. Echo posts are public: nearby people can read and relay them. Echo masks change daily; blocking a mask blocks future reception from that mask. Arena room announcements include a daily identifier, friend-recognition tag, game type and timestamp and are sent over a public internet channel. Your profile display name is not sent in room announcements. Deleting your copy cannot recall content another person has already saved or copied.

On-device content safety checks

Profile names, public posts, friend one-to-one text and names shown for first contacts are checked on this device with Korean/English phrase rules and a small classifier. Private group conversation text is checked on this device with the same rules as friend one-to-one text. Photos in direct and group conversations are checked on the device before sending and before display. Uncertain or failed checks hold sending and display. Unsupported formats, animations and transparent images are also held. Photos and conversations are not sent to a server or collected for training as part of these checks. Content hashes and decisions are kept in a bounded memory cache while the app runs. Hidden received originals may remain in encrypted device history and can be removed using message deletion. Filters can miss harmful content or restrict harmless content. Report evidence is shared externally only when you separately choose to include and send it.

Retention and disposal

Device data: Conversations, friends and identity remain until the relevant deletion/reset. Reset and account deletion also clear related avatars and game records. Non-identity choices such as language, accepted rules and the passed age threshold may remain. Incomplete received envelopes older than seven days are cleaned during startup/reception. Relay account: Authentication/connection data remains until deletion is processed. Verified owner deletion removes the account, role, registration state and queued mailbox. A minimal replay-prevention marker lasts 20 minutes and is then cleaned on a 60-second maintenance cycle. Offline mailbox: A seven-day expiry applies to disconnected persistent sessions. It is not a seven-day deadline measured from creation of every individual message. New service logs: Seven-day retention with hourly rotation/expiry, normally cleared within eight days. Rate-limit IPs are used only in a 60-second in-memory request window and cleaned every 60 seconds. Legacy test logs: The retention transition was applied on September 15, 2026; default system journals use a 30-day policy and inactive Mosquitto files expire 30 days after last modification. Including existing syslog rotations, pre-transition records are normally cleared within 40 days of transition (by October 25, 2026). Missed cleanup runs resume when the server restarts. Support/reports: Keep only as needed to handle the case, then delete within 30 days of completion. Unnecessary material is removed sooner. Operator-managed mail, attachments, trash and downloaded copies are included. Legally required exceptions identify their basis, scope and end date. Erasure: Electronic records, files, accounts and sessions are deleted or expired. Other users’ devices, exports, operating-system device transfers and providers’ independent records are separate. Uninstalling or deleting an account does not instantly physically erase all external copies. Rights/deletion requests for those records can be directed to the operator or the relevant provider. Limits for the new direct-report preparation: Unreceived local originals last up to seven days. Unnecessary material is removed sooner. Pending cases, retries and views do not extend deadlines. Accepting cancellation stops case access and further processing. If physical-file deletion fails, it enters a cleanup queue for retries and new intake is paused meanwhile. Terms of use

Service age

You must be at least 14 to use LATENT. Enter your birth date accurately. Identity creation and chat connections begin after the age check and acceptance of the usage rules. Registration with parental consent for users under 14 is not offered.

Prohibited conduct

Harassment, hate or discrimination, physical threats, scams, impersonation, exposing private information, sexual exploitation, illegal content and unsolicited advertising are prohibited. Respect other people’s rights and privacy, and their choice to end a conversation or game.

Using Echo

Echo is public to nearby users. Do not post contacts, home addresses, recovery keys or sensitive information. Bypassing public safety filters or changing identities to evade another person’s block is prohibited.

Protect your identity

Anyone with your recovery key can use the same identity. Never share recovery keys or transfer QR codes with others. A friend QR contains a public contact, not your recovery key. Verify friends using a QR code or identity fingerprint.

Connection and storage limits

Delivery depends on distance, Bluetooth availability, operating-system background restrictions and internet conditions. Delivery times and continuous connections are not guaranteed. LATENT does not replace emergency communication services. Keep your own copies of important information.

Reporting and action

Hold a message to report or block its sender. Blocking applies to future reception on this device. The operator reviews received public-post reports and appeals, checking one signed original and minimal information to decide short-term actions or revocations for the exact post or daily mask. This does not guarantee deletion on every device or permanent exclusion of someone returning with a new identity. The operator cannot recall all distributed offline copies. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in reports. Saving a report on this device does not mean the operator received it.

Child safety standards

No child sexual abuse or exploitation

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), text promoting exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation and exploitation transactions are strictly prohibited. Requesting, promoting, sharing or linking to this content is also prohibited.

How to report

Hold the message to report or block, and contact support. Do not download or forward illegal images or videos. You can report using a description and the subject’s identifier without attaching content. Contact local emergency services if someone is in immediate danger.

Operator response

The operator reviews received reports and takes action required by applicable law within its control. Confirmed child sexual abuse material is reported to the appropriate authorities where legally required, with cooperation on valid requests. The operator cannot recall all distributed copies or completely prevent someone from returning under a new identity. Contact support for a response or appeal.
