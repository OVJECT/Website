Request deletion without the app

You can request deletion by email even if you cannot use the app. Include the app name LATENT, the public identity ID concerned, and the scope of your request. Never send recovery keys, passwords or private keys. Requests require verification of ownership; you cannot delete someone else’s account.

Deletion scope and retention

Device data: Conversations, friends and identity remain until the relevant deletion/reset. Reset and account deletion also clear related avatars and game records. Non-identity choices such as language, accepted rules and the passed age threshold may remain. Incomplete received envelopes older than seven days are cleaned during startup/reception. Relay account: Authentication/connection data remains until deletion is processed. Verified owner deletion removes the account, role, registration state and queued mailbox. A minimal replay-prevention marker lasts 20 minutes and is then cleaned on a 60-second maintenance cycle. Offline mailbox: A seven-day expiry applies to disconnected persistent sessions. It is not a seven-day deadline measured from creation of every individual message. New service logs: Seven-day retention with hourly rotation/expiry, normally cleared within eight days. Rate-limit IPs are used only in a 60-second in-memory request window and cleaned every 60 seconds. Legacy test logs: The retention transition was applied on September 15, 2026; default system journals use a 30-day policy and inactive Mosquitto files expire 30 days after last modification. Including existing syslog rotations, pre-transition records are normally cleared within 40 days of transition (by October 25, 2026). Missed cleanup runs resume when the server restarts. Support/reports: Keep only as needed to handle the case, then delete within 30 days of completion. Unnecessary material is removed sooner. Operator-managed mail, attachments, trash and downloaded copies are included. Legally required exceptions identify their basis, scope and end date. Erasure: Electronic records, files, accounts and sessions are deleted or expired. Other users’ devices, exports, operating-system device transfers and providers’ independent records are separate. Uninstalling or deleting an account does not instantly physically erase all external copies. Rights/deletion requests for those records can be directed to the operator or the relevant provider. Limits for the new direct-report preparation: Unreceived local originals last up to seven days. After receipt, local originals are removed and minimal receipt details/secrets remain until server expiry, at most sixty days. Server originals/attachments/free text expire at the earlier of first receipt +30 days or closure +7 days; minimal state, evidence/target verification and authentication hashes at receipt +60 days or closure +30 days. Unnecessary material is removed sooner. Pending cases, retries and views do not extend deadlines. Accepting cancellation stops case access and further processing. If physical-file deletion fails, it enters a cleanup queue for retries and new intake is paused meanwhile. A cancelled status does not confirm completed physical erasure; minimal cancellation records retain the original deadline. Appeal notes last at most thirty days and are deleted on closure; minimal appeal state expires at receipt +30 days or closure +7 days, whichever is earlier. Pending notifications last up to seven days and are removed sooner on success, cancellation, expiry or the attempt limit. This does not recall delivered Gmail copies. Generic notifications without case details are distinct from directly submitted support mail/attachments. Operator-controlled inbox, sent, trash and downloaded copies are reviewed and cleaned; replacing the notification format does not automatically delete earlier copies. Statutory security records are installed and maintained separately from case evidence. Administrator access audits follow the applicable one/two-year rules, with a conservative 732-day operating default; permission grant/change/removal history follows the initial register and internal procedure, retaining each record for at least three years. Only administrator, time, action, access path and minimum references are recorded, never originals, reasons, case secrets or private keys. Case references and pseudonymous daily-mask audit references follow this separate security period without extending originals, secrets or complete case-state records. HMAC references are not described as anonymized. This does not extend raw-case retention. Originals, attachments, notes and per-case receipt databases are excluded from routine bulk backups. Minimal configuration/public-decision backups do not extend included record deadlines. Decisions, withdrawals and trust-key state follow separate validity/replay-prevention lifetimes. Actual files, database/WAL, caches, temporary uploads and backups require erasure and recovery-prevention checks; accepted cancellation and completed physical erasure are distinguished, and failed deletions are tracked for retry. Friend reports use the same direct-report original/note, minimal-state and cancellation deadlines; attachment-off still retains the note and case state under those deadlines. Operator-downloaded copies used for photo review follow the same final original-data deadline; downloads are audited separately and do not extend retention.
